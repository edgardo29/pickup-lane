## What information Pickup Lane uses

Pickup Lane needs account information such as your name, email address, date of birth, and any phone or home-location details you choose to provide. We use a birth date to determine whether you meet the minimum age of 18. Firebase handles sign-in credentials and email verification; Pickup Lane keeps the linked account and profile information needed for the service.

When you use the service, we keep information about games you create or join, bookings, guests, waitlists, Need a Sub posts and requests, messages, notification choices, and account or staff actions that affect you. Game listings and posts contain details you provide, such as location, schedule, description, and organizer payment instructions. Public summaries and information available to signed-in or connected participants differ by feature; do not put information in a listing or message that you do not intend to share with its permitted audience.

For official-game purchases and community-game publishing fees, Stripe handles card entry and payment-provider transactions. Pickup Lane keeps card summaries and references to saved payment methods, together with booking, charge, refund, and game-credit records. Pickup Lane does not store complete payment-card details. Community-game player payments and Need a Sub payments occur outside Pickup Lane and are not processed as in-app player charges.

## Why information is used and who receives it

We use information to create and protect accounts; show games and posts; manage participation, bookings, payments, credits, and refunds; send service notices; support users; review safety and conduct concerns; and meet approved financial, security, and legal obligations. Authorized staff may access the information needed for support, moderation, financial review, and account actions.

The planned service uses Render and PostgreSQL for application records, Firebase for authentication, Stripe for payment processing, Cloudflare R2 for venue images, Postmark for transactional email, Grafana Cloud for operational monitoring, PostHog for selected product analytics, and Amazon S3 for independent backups. Each provider receives only information needed for its approved purpose. **[Privacy review placeholder: confirm the final processor list, each disclosure, and its retention and deletion treatment before publication.]**

Selected product analytics are collected only after the visitor gives the required affirmative permission. The planned events concern game discovery and views, completed registration, community-game creation, and completed bookings. Withholding or withdrawing required permission stops further analytics collection for that visitor. Automatic click and form tracking and session recording are outside the approved analytics scope.

## Your choices and account deletion

Your account lets you change certain profile information, notification email preferences, and saved cards, and request deletion. An accepted deletion request blocks ordinary protected account activity while cleanup proceeds. Information that must remain for a specifically approved payment, refund, dispute, fraud, safety, audit, or legal obligation is restricted to that purpose and does not keep unrelated profile details. Some processor or backup records may need separate treatment before deletion is complete.

## Adults only and policy updates

Accounts and game participation are for adults at least 18. If Pickup Lane determines that an account holder is under 18, access is restricted and the information collected about that person is handled under the applicable approved deletion or legally required restricted-retention process.
