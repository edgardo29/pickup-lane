## Before you play

Pickup Lane accounts and participation are limited to adults at least 18. Read the listing for the particular game before joining. It identifies the date, time, location, format, player group, skill level, indoor or outdoor setting, and whether the game is official or independently organized by a community host. Review any host rules or instructions that apply to that game.

## Soccer and venue risks

Soccer involves running, quick changes of direction, contact with other players, balls and equipment, and use of a playing surface that can change with weather or use. Falls, collisions, and other injuries can occur. Outdoor weather and unsafe field conditions may cause a game to be cancelled; an indoor venue may also become unavailable or unsafe.

Consider your own condition and the conditions at the venue before taking part. The fact that a game is listed, or that a booking is confirmed, does not mean it is free from injury risk.

## Who organizes the activity

Pickup Lane runs **official games**, initially in Illinois. An independent organizer runs each **community game** and is responsible for the arrangements that organizer makes with players, including any off-app player payment. Need a Sub posts concern games outside Pickup Lane and do not turn those games into Pickup Lane official or community bookings.

Game-specific cancellation and payment terms are in the [Cancellation & Refund Policy](cancellation-refund-policy.md) and, for community games, in any terms the organizer provides. This acknowledgment does not promise an injury-related refund or change those terms.
