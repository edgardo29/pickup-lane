## Using Pickup Lane

Pickup Lane helps adults find and participate in soccer games. People across the United States may register and organize community games. Pickup Lane initially organizes its own **official games in Illinois**. A game listing identifies which kind of game it is before you join.

You must be at least 18 to have an account or participate in a game. Some activities, including hosting, joining, booking, payment, and private messaging, also require a verified email address. Keep your account information accurate and do not use another person's account.

## Official and community games

Pickup Lane organizes official games and handles their in-app bookings and payments. The price, available places, guest options, and applicable game terms are shown before you confirm. A place is confirmed only when the booking process reports confirmation; a payment still being processed does not establish a confirmed place.

An independent organizer runs each community game and supplies its game details and any payment instructions. Players arrange community-game payments directly with that organizer, outside Pickup Lane. Pickup Lane does not collect those player payments or process or guarantee their refunds. Payment disputes and refund arrangements remain between the player and the **original organizer**, including if the game is cancelled after staff remove a suspended organizer. Any Pickup Lane fee charged to a community organizer for publishing a game is separate from what players pay that organizer.

Need a Sub posts concern soccer games outside Pickup Lane. They let adults seek or offer substitute places; they are not Pickup Lane game bookings or in-app player payments. A stated price for such a post is due at the venue, not through Pickup Lane.

## Participation and conduct

Soccer involves physical activity and possible injury. Read the [Participation & Risk Acknowledgment](participation-risk-acknowledgment.md) and the particular game's details before deciding whether to take part. That draft acknowledgment does not itself establish a waiver or a separate acceptance requirement.

Game listings, host instructions, posts, and messages must be used for their intended game-related purpose. Pickup Lane may review content and take the account or game actions described in the service, including restricting new participation, hiding content, or cancelling a game when appropriate. Suspending an account does not by itself cancel its existing bookings or hosted games; existing commitments may still be managed or withdrawn under the applicable rules.

## Payments, cancellations, and refunds

Official-game checkout may use a saved card, available Pickup Lane game credit, or both. A paid official-game waitlist requires separate consent for a possible later charge; joining the waitlist alone does not charge you. Additional paid guests require payment before they are confirmed.

The [Cancellation & Refund Policy](cancellation-refund-policy.md) explains the established differences between official games, community games, changed official-game details, waitlists, and guests. Check the terms shown for your particular game before confirming. Community organizers may supply their own cancellation terms; when none are supplied, confirm payment and refund arrangements with the organizer before paying them.
