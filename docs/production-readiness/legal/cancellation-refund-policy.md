## Official games: player cancellation

If you cancel your official-game place at least **24 hours before the scheduled start**, you may be eligible for a refund or Pickup Lane game credit. A later cancellation may not be refunded. Removing a guest follows the applicable game policy. Cancelling your account releases future places but does **not**, by itself, issue a refund or game credit.

## If Pickup Lane cancels an official game

When Pickup Lane cancels an official game, eligible paid value is returned through a refund and/or restoration of game credit used for that booking. For a booking paid with both cash and Pickup Lane credit, the applicable cash amount is refunded through the original payment method and used credit is restored. A payment or refund still being processed must not be presented as completed.

Unsafe weather or field conditions may cause an outdoor game to be cancelled. Cancellation and its applicable money outcome are communicated to affected players; a weather concern alone does not automatically issue a refund or credit.

## If official-game details change

If changed official-game details no longer work for you, you may request removal and choose a refund to your original payment method or Pickup Lane game credit, including after the ordinary cancellation cutoff. Your booking remains confirmed when you submit the request. Removal and the financial relief occur only when the request is processed; the edit or request alone does not return money.

If you paid partly with cash and partly with existing game credit, choosing the original payment method returns the eligible cash-paid portion to that method and restores the previously used credit. Choosing game credit provides the full eligible amount as game credit, without also restoring the old credit on top of that full amount. Cash refunded cannot exceed the cash actually paid, after accounting for prior refunds. Existing refunds, credits, and paid guests are considered before the outcome is completed.

## Waitlists and guests

Joining a paid official-game waitlist does not charge you. A saved card and separate consent are needed before a possible later automatic charge if a place opens for your whole party. You may leave before an offer without a charge. A required payment must succeed before the booking is confirmed. Paid additional guests likewise require payment before confirmation. Guest cancellation follows the policy applicable to that game.

## Community games and Need a Sub

Community-game player payments happen directly between players and the independent organizer, outside Pickup Lane. If the organizer provided cancellation or refund terms, review those terms for the game. If no terms were supplied, confirm arrangements with the organizer before paying; Pickup Lane does not promise a refund. Any payment dispute or refund remains between you and the **original organizer**, including if staff cancel a game after removing a suspended host. Pickup Lane does not process or guarantee community-player refunds or issue them as Pickup Lane game credits.

For Need a Sub, any stated price is due at the outside venue. Pickup Lane does not process that player payment or provide an in-app refund for it.

The fee a community organizer may pay Pickup Lane to publish a game is separate from player payments. A cancellation can require a staff decision about that fee; this policy does not promise an automatic publish-fee refund or credit.
